# Use Claude as your helper

1. Pick a project from the [main list](../README.md).
2. Open its **Copy the Claude prompt** link.
3. Copy the text in the box and paste it into your Claude chat.

Ask Claude to give you **one step at a time**. Try each step before moving on.

If you get stuck, paste the error message and ask:

> I'm a beginner. What does this mean, and what should I do next?

Once the example works, ask for one small change, such as adding a new spending category.

Use the sample data while learning. Remove personal details before sharing your own files with Claude.
